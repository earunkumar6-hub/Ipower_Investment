# IPower Investments CRM

Next.js 15 + React 19 + Prisma 6 + Postgres. Company "IPower Investments" (originally specced as FL_CRM).
Pending change requests from the owner: `docs/Changes.txt`. Run/deploy steps: `README.md`, `deploy/INSTALL.md`.

## How to work here
- Plan first and confirm in chat before changing files; ask when a request is ambiguous.
- Make targeted changes; don't rewrite working code.
- Never touch `.env` or secrets without asking.

## Pinned on purpose
- Prisma 6.19.3 (not 7/8). TanStack Table v8 (v9 has a different API). exceljs 4.4.0.
- Forms submit with `submitKeepingValues` (`src/lib/form.ts`) so React 19 doesn't wipe input on validation errors.

## Business rules decided by the owner
- Day Book is the money ledger: every movement is an immutable entry, corrections are reversals, closed days accept nothing (`src/lib/daybook.ts`). Balances are summed from the first entry, so never post before the last closed day.
- Receipts: bill date (`receiptDate`) drives Day Book, FY, instalment paid-on and RD commission; transaction date (`txnDate`) is when the customer paid and is only stored/printed. Past dates allowed only after the last closed day.
- Several dues paid together → one `PAY-` Payment with one RCPT per due (RD deposit, commission, Day Book stay per RCPT); a single due stays a plain RCPT. Payable dues come from `src/lib/dues.ts`: oldest first, advance up to 12 months within the term; the server re-derives them from `{policyId, count}` and creates advance instalments (as Paid) only when paid. A payment is cancelled whole, never one RCPT of it, and not once an RD line is deposited.
- P&L income = E-Sevai service charges + Other income (Day Book entry type `OtherIncome`, Admin/Super Admin, with a reason) + "Commission earned (consolidated)" from Commission received, for the month it is for. The expected RD commission recorded on receipts is still shown and counted nowhere.
- Commission received (#18, `/commission`, `CommissionReceipt`, `Commission.View` = Super Admin only): source (list + typed Other), for month, amount, bank account, UTR (required), date received, note; cancel with reason. Owner-only ledger, NOT in the Day Book – bank balances / tallies Admins see leave it out (owner, 22 Oct 2026). Cards (this month, this FY, entries this month), by month / by FY, Excel. Its change-log lines are hidden from Admins.
- Post office schemes: RD, TD, MIS, SCSS, SSA, PPF, NSC, KVP. RD monthly and SSA/PPF yearly are dues collected via Receipts; TD/MIS/SCSS/NSC/KVP are one-time, so Renewals shows a read-only maturity reminder (start + term), kept out of totals and the Day Book, closed with "Mark matured". Renewals filter: product → company (insurer/AMC) → scheme (`src/lib/renewals.ts`).
- Paid to company: Renewals Paid tab shows whether collected money reached the company. RD = PO deposit in the Day Book (unchanged); insurance, MF, SSA, PPF = tick receipts of one company → "Pay to company" (`CompanyPayment`, date + company receipt/UTR + cash/bank account, Day Book `PaidToCompany` money-out). A receipt paid to the company can't be cancelled. No undo yet. The same can be done for one receipt from its page ("Update deposit status": insurance/MF/SSA/PPF via `payToCompany`; RD via `depositOneRdReceipt`, a one-line PO deposit from cash or bank that skips the UPI vs ATM check). Status text, filter and "Collected by" (receipt `createdById`) / "· by <user>" (who recorded the deposit) come from `src/lib/deposit-status.ts`.
- Payment modes (`src/lib/payment-options.ts`, `PaymentFields` component): Cash needs a note/coin denomination that adds up to the amount (stored as JSON, printed); UPI/Cheque/Card need the reference; Net banking needs NEFT / IMPS / RTGS (`netType`), the customer's bank (list in `PaymentOption`, kind Bank – reference only) + transaction ID. Modes can be switched off (`PaymentOption` kind Mode). "Received by" (`createdById`) and the BL- bill number are printed on receipt, payment and direct bill PDFs.
- Every due (`RenewalItem`) has a BL- bill number from the DB sequence `bill_no_seq` (seed resets it). Owner-paid works for every billed product except mutual funds and one-time deposits (`OWNER_PAYABLE`); RD commission only for RD.
- Received into (change set #3): every non-cash receipt and direct bill names the active company bank account the money came into (`intoAccountId` → `checkPayment` → `receivingAccount` in `src/lib/accounts.ts`); cash always goes to the cash drawer. Lists, detail pages and exports show it as "SBI ••1234" (`accountLabel`), switched-off accounts included.
- A bill paid as part of a PAY- payment shows the PAY- number everywhere a paid bill shows its receipt (Renewals Paid tab, customer page, exports) via `receiptRef` in `src/lib/payment-lines.ts`.
- Receipts filters (`receiptFilter` in `src/lib/lists.ts`, shared by the screen and the export): name/mobile, CIF, product, policy no., mode, account, status, bill date from/to, deposit status. AND-combined, server-side, on every tab; tab counts use `count()` with the same where. Users only see receipts they entered; owner-paid slips ignore mode/account/deposit and filter on the date recorded.
- One receipt for many bills (`Payment`, PAY- numbers, `kind`): Customer (several dues of one customer), Combined (`/receipts/combined/new`: bills already raised, ticked across any customers – a User only their own customers – made out to a "Paid by" name + mobile, optional PO receipt no.), BillGroup (`/receipts/new?groupId=`: every member's bills incl. advance). Combined / bill-group payments have no `customerId`; every bill keeps its own RCPT line under its own customer. Shared code: `src/lib/receipt-lines.ts` (`pickDues` re-derives dues from `{policyId, count}`, tenant + `canActOn` per policy, locks policies in id order; `payDueLine`). Visibility: `canSeePayment` (Admin/SA, who entered it, or owner of a line's customer). Cancel is whole-payment as before.
- Bill groups (`/receipts/groups`, `BillGroup` / `BillGroupMember`): create / add / remove = `BillGroup.Manage` (Admin, Super Admin), logged. A policy can be in only one group (unique `policyId`). One-time deposits can't join (`joinProblem` in group-actions.ts). Add by exact policy no., or "Find policies to add" (customer name / Customer ID / mobile / part of the policy no. → tick → "Add selected", all or none). Family grouped receipts tab: "Pick a bill group" + "New family receipt" (groups with policies). The group receipt lists members with nothing to pay as grey lines "Nothing to pay · paid up to …" (`showSettled`, `PolicyOption.paidUntil`).
- Instalments by date (`BillsPicker` on the customer and bill-group receipt): From / To → "Show instalments" ticks every bill up to To (older unpaid ones are included – oldest first). "Generate late bill" = PDF of overdue bills (`/api/instalment-bills?kind=late`); "Generate advance bills" (`generateAdvanceBills`) raises advance instalments up to To as Due bills with BL- numbers (no payment; same 12-month / bill-rule / term limits) + PDF. `RenewalItem` is unique on (policyId, dueDate).
- Receipts tabs: Customer receipts (RCPTs not inside a Combined / BillGroup payment), Owner-paid receipts (`tab=owner`), Combined receipts, Family grouped receipts – each with a count from the same filters; export has a sheet per tab.
- Day Book transfers (`Contra`, two lines, shown as one row "₹X cash / ₹X bank"): ATM withdrawal (bank account picker, transaction no. required, withdrawn by any user / owner, reason, cash notes) and Deposit to bank (bank account, slip / txn no., cash notes). Never income or expense.
- Owner withdrawal / contribution (`OwnerEntry`, entry types `OwnerWithdrawal` / `OwnerContribution`): anyone records; posted to the Day Book at once; Pending → Confirmed or Rejected by the Super Admin only (`Owner.Confirm`; reject needs a reason and reverses the Day Book line dated today). Bank needs a txn no., cash needs notes. Not in P&L totals; listed in the P&L "Owner statement" (and its export sheet).
- Grace period (`src/lib/daybook-grace.ts`, `Tenant.dayCloseGraceDays`, default 3): badge "OPEN · grace day X of N" on days with entries; past grace an Admin's close is refused (UI + server), the Super Admin can close (`DayBook.CloseLate`). Only closing is locked – posting to the day is not (owner, 22 Oct 2026). Alert = dashboard action "Day Book past grace period" + red banner on the Day Book (no message sent).
- E-Sevai / CSC entry (#7): Date + Time (`txnTime` "HH:MM" IST, now by default, not later than now on today; older entries show createdAt via `serviceTime`). Date (today by default; earlier days allowed, not before the last closed day, never future – drives the Day Book date; "Today's entries" also lists back-dated ones entered today); service EB / PAN / Certificate / Other (typed name, `ServiceType` OTHER); service no. (`providerRef`), transaction no. (`providerTxnNo`, optional – owner, 2 Nov 2026), mobile, name, gender, amount paid, paid from (any active wallet, or GPay / Net banking direct from a bank account where `allowBankPay`), collected (prefilled from the rate table, staff may change – the old EB gap no longer blocks), Cash (notes must add up) or UPI (received-into bank + optional UTR), notes. Service charge = collected − paid (P&L). A mobile matching a customer links the entry (`customerId`) and shows "Matches customer … (CIF)". "Save and print receipt" opens `/api/esevai/[id]/pdf`.
- Wallets (#6): CSC / E-Sevai / Other wallets (old EB / PAN wallets became Other, balances kept). Recharge = Admin / Super Admin, from a company bank account, transaction no. required, logged. Statement per wallet (`/esevai/wallets/[id]`, date range, running balance). Wallets can be switched off any time (entries pick their wallet). Statement upload waits for a sample provider file.
- Joint account (#12): proposal panel – first holder = the proposal's customer (CIF, or the customer matched by mobile, or "given on issue"); second holder = existing customer (search name / CIF / mobile, `searchCustomers`) or a new person (name + mobile; a customer with that mobile is linked instead). Panel "Account holders": Holder CIF for every proposal (single or joint) plus Second holder CIF when joint – CIFs given by the post office / insurer (`firstHolderCif`, `secondHolderCif`, typed like the policy number, optional until issue, copied to the policy; "Customer ID" = this CRM's own CIF-000… number). Saved by `saveJointAccount` (logged, locked after issue). On issue a new second holder becomes a customer; `Policy.secondHolderId` set. Lists show "A + B (joint)" (`holdersLabel`). The second holder's customer page lists it under "Joint accounts – second holder"; bills / receipts / renewals stay with the first holder. Aadhaar / PAN duplicate check (#28) not applied yet.
- Aadhaar / PAN / company CIF unique (#1, #13, #28, `src/lib/identity.ts`): "Customer consent taken" tick (who / when on Proposal + Customer) is required before Aadhaar / PAN are saved (proposal panel and customer page "Add Aadhaar / PAN"); issuing is not blocked by it (owner, 22 Oct 2026). Full numbers stored AES-GCM encrypted (`aadhaarEnc` / `panEnc`) with a keyed HMAC fingerprint (`aadhaarHash` / `panHash`, unique per tenant on Customer); screens, logs and exports show only masked forms. A number that belongs to another customer or another person's open proposal is refused with `ActionResult.duplicate` → `DuplicateCustomerDialog` (Customer ID, name, masked mobile, matched on, products; "Open existing customer" / "Go back and correct", no override). Company CIF unique within each company (partner), same check on Account holders. Issue copies numbers + consent to the customer and refuses a mismatch. Older records have masked values only, so they are checked once re-entered.
- Roles and access (Settings → Roles and access, Super Admin only = `Access.Manage`, fixed): tick boxes per permission for Admin and User (`PERMISSION_GROUPS`); only differences from the built-in defaults (`ROLE_PERMISSIONS`) are stored (`RolePermission`); `hasPermission(role, p)` reads them via `loadAccess` (src/lib/access.ts, 5 s in-memory cache, cleared on save; single-agency install). Super Admin always has everything. "See every staff member's records" = `Data.SeeAll` (what `seesAll` checks). Reset to defaults; all changes logged (entity Access).
- Users (Settings → Users, Super Admin only): add a login (name, username, role User / Admin, password ≥ 8, hashed by better-auth), change role, login on / off (off = signed out at once and refused at sign-in by the better-auth session hook), set a new password (signs the person out). Delete (type DELETE): their customers, leads, policies, proposals, quotations and open calls are handed over to a staff member picked in the dialog, then the login is removed (password + sessions deleted, username freed, `User.deletedAt`); the name stays on old records. The owner's own login can't be changed or deleted there. `getSessionUser` reads role / active from the database on each request; last sign-in recorded.
- Direct bills (`/direct-bills`, DB- numbers): anyone (customer or walk-in name + mobile) for a purpose; Day Book `Collection` in, product category "Direct bill" (not P&L income); linked to the customer whose mobile matches, and shown on that customer's page under "Linked by mobile number". Same back-dating rule as receipts. Admin/Super Admin cancel with reason → reversal, number kept.
- Post office verification (`/receipts/verification`, any role with `DayBook.Create`): RD PO deposits by PO receipt no., post office company payments by policy no. → Verified (final; RD receipts Deposited → Verified) or Mismatch (note required, can be re-checked). Logged.
- Settings → Bank accounts and wallets (`Account`): bank details (bank, branch, account no. AES-GCM encrypted + `last4`, IFSC, MICR 9 digits, Savings/Current); wallets CSC / E-Sevai / Other (older EB/PAN/certificate wallets have no `walletKind`). Never deleted: `active=false` hides an account from new entries (pickers and server checks use `active: true`), old entries keep it. One default bank (`isDefault`) is where money lands on screens without an account picker (`cashAndBank()` in `src/lib/accounts.ts`); the default and office cash can't be switched off. Opening balance only when adding.
- Settings → Payment modes (`PaymentOption`: modes on/off, at least one on; customer bank list), Expense purposes (`DropdownOption` list `expensePurpose`, case-insensitive, removing hides it from new entries; "Other" always offered), Day Book (`Tenant.dayCloseGraceDays`, default 3 – enforcement comes with the Day Book phase). All logged.
- Expenses: Notes required (UI + server; older expenses have none); purpose must be in the list or "Other"; "From account" = any active bank account or office cash. Repayments still use office cash / the default bank.
- Products: "Can be closed during the lock-in period" (`closeDuringLockIn`, only with premature closure allowed, never MF) lets "Close early" skip the lock-in check. Bank fields on products include MICR and account type.
- Overdue text: one formatter `overdueText` (`src/lib/overdue.ts`) – up to 30 days in days, then months of 30 days plus the days left (31 → 1 month 1 day, 35 → 1 month 5 days, 60 → 2 months); used on Renewals, Dashboard, customer page and exports.
- Cash denomination: `CashDenominationGrid` / `DenominationInputs` (`src/components/cash-denomination-grid.tsx`) on every form where cash moves; the server checks it with `checkDenominations` (total must equal the amount). Stored on Receipt/Payment/DirectBill, on Expense (paid later at approval) and on the Day Book cash line (`DayBookEntry.denominations`) for PO deposits, pay to company, return to owner, expense payments/repayments. ATM, deposit to bank, owner entries (Day Book phase) and E-Sevai get it when those forms are rebuilt.
- "Other" rule: `OtherOptionSelect` / `OtherSelect` (`src/components/other-option-select.tsx`) + `resolveDropdown` (`src/lib/dropdowns.ts`): Other needs typed text, which is saved on the entry and added to the list as "added by staff" (case-insensitive, once). A value an Admin removed stays off the list even if typed again. Lists: expense purposes, quotation products, file types per record (Settings → Dropdown values). Gender "Other" is a plain option.
- Long forms (customer edit, add product) have Save at the top and bottom, both through the check-before-saving pop-up.
- Customers have a CIF number (`cifNo`, CIF-000124, DB sequence `cif_no_seq` via `next_cif_no()`, unique per tenant, seed resets it): shown on the customer header, list (searchable) and export. Duplicate blocking by Aadhaar/PAN (#13/#28) is NOT built – waits for the owner's decision on storing full numbers.
- Customer photo: JPG/PNG ≤ 2 MB in `storage/photos`, served by `/api/customers/[id]/photo` after the owner check; initials when none. Logged.
- Follow-ups on the customer page: calls about the customer (`Call.customerId`, kind "customer"), its renewals and proposals; badge "Follow-up due …" for the next open one; "Add follow-up" books a call for the customer's owner, which joins "calls open" and the reminder pop-up; ends with Done or No answer (next call).
- Premature closure button (`closureButton` in `src/lib/closure.ts`): hidden with "Not allowed", greyed out until the lock-in ends (unless the product allows closing during lock-in), or active; mutual funds always Withdraw.
- EB bills ₹1,000–1,999 and ₹30,000+: refused with "rate not configured" (no placeholder rate).
- Excel/PDF export needs `Report.Export`; mobiles in exports stay masked.
- Settings → Products and schemes (`Settings.Edit`, Admin + Super Admin): products with bill rule, pay modes, lock-in, bank fields, premature closure, shown/required fields, custom fields; schemes with tenure and rate history (`rateOn`, `src/lib/products.ts`). Policies link via `productId`/`schemeId` (null for older ones).
- Policy closure: "Close early" (date + reason, refused inside lock-in or if the product forbids it) or MF "Withdraw" (date) → status PrematureClosed/Withdrawn, unpaid instalments → `Stopped`, no Day Book money.
- Change log: `audit()` (`src/lib/audit.ts`) in the same transaction as the change; Settings → Change log (`Audit.View`). New modules should log their changes too.
- Leads: status is only Interested / Not interested (reason, closes calls) / Call back (needs a call); `stage` (Lead → Quotation → Proposal → Customer) moves forward only via `advanceStage` (`src/lib/leads.ts`). Required: first name, DOB, gender, ≥1 mobile, status, business type (`LeadSource`), referrer for Referral. All numbers live in `LeadMobile` (unique per tenant → duplicate check); `Lead.mobile`/`onWhatsApp` mirror the first. `name` = first + last.
- Calls (follow-ups) are about a lead, a renewal (`policyId`) or a proposal; renewal/proposal calls end with `Done`. Follow-ups screen and export share `loadFollowUps` (`src/lib/followups.ts`). Forms that need "Check before saving" use `useConfirmBeforeSave` (`src/components/confirm-save.tsx`).
- Quotations: no GST anywhere – total = premium; the GST columns were dropped and existing quotes / proposals / policies / unpaid dues were stripped of the 18% (owner, 29 Oct 2026, migration `20261029120000_remove_gst`). Each product has a decision (Selected → one proposal each / Not accepted); "Mark not accepted" needs a reason; delete is a soft delete (only while no proposal) and is logged. Image export = `htmlToPng` of the PDF HTML.
- Proposals hold the policy details (pay mode, term, start date, premium paying term, scheme…); `createPolicyWithSchedule` (`src/lib/policies.ts`) builds the policy and renewals for both issued proposals and "Add product directly". Renewals are the payments after the first and end before the term (Yearly × 3 years = 2). No OTP consent step at all and Aadhaar / PAN / KYC documents are optional (owner, 20 Oct 2026): a proposal needs only pay mode + start date before the policy number; Aadhaar / PAN (masked) can be entered any time until issued. Accounts keep `rateAtOpen`.
- Bank details are AES-256-GCM encrypted with `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (`src/lib/crypto.ts`); losing the key makes them unreadable.
- Attachments (quotation / proposal / customer) live in `storage/attachments`, served only by `/api/attachments/[id]` after the record's owner check; uploader or Admin removes (logged). PDF / JPG / PNG / WEBP up to 5 MB, except Aadhaar / PAN copies: a JPG of 50 KB or smaller only (owner, 26 Oct 2026) – the panel shrinks any photo picked to such a JPG in the browser, the server refuses anything else (`src/lib/attachment-rules.ts`). The panel also stops other files over 5 MB before sending: the server-action body limit is 6 MB and a bigger upload would show the Next.js error screen.
- Customers have several numbers (`CustomerMobile`, not unique – family members may share); `Customer.mobile` is the first. Customer 360 (`src/lib/customer-360.ts`) feeds the page and its export: holdings with lock-in / maturity / masked bank details, E-Sevai entries linked by any number, recent activity. Family grouping is deferred (owner, 19 Oct).
- Renewals: date range From/To or quick pick (`renewalRange` in lists.ts; Due defaults to next 30 days, Paid last 30). Tabs: Due / Overdue / Paid / By customer – the Not renewed tab and its card were removed (owner, 2 Nov 2026). Bill rules live in `billEnd` / `fullSchedule` (`src/lib/policies.ts`) and drive stored renewals, advance dues (`payableDues` `billUntil`) and the schedule page. `refreshOverdue` also marks billed policies Matured after maturity when nothing is unpaid; one-time PO deposits wait for "Mark matured". Maturity reminders show a month before for every scheme with a maturity (owner, 20 Oct 2026); health (UntilClosed) has none. "Not renewed" button on Overdue rows lapses the policy (reason, logged); lapsed policies show on the customer page.
- Round-2 plan and owner decisions: `docs/changes_round2.json`.
- Letterhead (logo, company, tagline, "R. Murali, Financial Advisor") lives in `src/lib/brand.ts`. Logo = client file of 19 Oct 2026 (cropped, transparent, only ~224px – ask for a larger copy before print work).
- Colours follow the logo: `brand-*` = crimson (#D22229; buttons, links, '--primary'), `saffron-*` = saffron (#FAA618; selected menu item, highlights, notes), sidebar `brand-800`. Status badges keep their meaning colours. Don't use Tailwind blue/indigo for UI chrome.

## Tests – they write to the database in `DATABASE_URL`
- `pnpm test` (vitest) and `pnpm e2e` (Playwright, reseeds) use the dev DB from `.env`. Don't run them against real data.
- Use a scratch DB: `docker exec app-postgres-1 createdb -U flcrm flcrm_check`, set
  `DATABASE_URL=postgresql://flcrm:flcrm@localhost:5433/flcrm_check`, `pnpm prisma migrate deploy`, `pnpm db:seed`, `pnpm test`, then `dropdb`.
- Dev Postgres is on host port 5433. Seed tests that check month totals only run from the 10th of the month.
